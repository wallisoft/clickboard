# Commercial licensing

Clickboard is free for individuals and small businesses under the [PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## Who needs a commercial licence?

Under that licence, an organisation qualifies as a small business if, together with its affiliates, it has **fewer than 100 people** (employees and contractors) and **less than US$1,000,000 in annual revenue**. Personal, non-commercial use is always free.

If your organisation is larger than that and uses Clickboard, you need a commercial licence from Wallisoft.

## What you get

- The right to use Clickboard across your organisation.
- Priority support by email.
- Pricing that scales with the number of machines.

Email **hello@tiny-web.uk** with your organisation's name and roughly how many machines you'd like to use Clickboard on, and we'll send a quote.
