# Contributing to Clickboard

Contributions are very welcome, especially a macOS port, fixes for other Linux desktops, and translations.

## Before you start

- For anything bigger than a small fix, please open an issue first so we can agree on the approach.
- Keep pull requests focused on one change.
- Test on the platforms your change touches, and say in the pull request what you tested on.

## The Contributor Licence Agreement

The first time you open a pull request, a bot will ask you to sign our [Contributor Licence Agreement](CLA.md) by posting a one-line comment. You only do this once.

We want to be upfront about why. Clickboard is free for individuals and small businesses, and Wallisoft also builds **commercial products on top of Clickboard's core**, such as a planned keyboard-and-mouse sharing product, and sells commercial licences to larger organisations. The CLA lets us include your contribution in those products as well as in Clickboard itself. You keep the copyright in your work, and you're free to use it however you like.

If that doesn't sit right with you, no hard feelings: you're still welcome to open issues, suggest ideas and share bug reports.

## Security issues

Please don't report security problems in a public issue. Email **hello@tiny-web.uk** instead, and we'll respond as quickly as we can.
